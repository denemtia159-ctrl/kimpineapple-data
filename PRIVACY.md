# Privacy Policy - Kimpineapple_data

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Kimpineapple_data is a personal, single-user automation (n8n running on the owner's own PC) that organizes the owner's game-community data files into the owner's own Google Drive.

## Data accessed
- Google Drive: the app creates folders and uploads files only inside the owner's Drive folder "FPS스트리머이동". It does not read, modify, or delete other files.

- ## Data use and sharing
- - Data is used solely to store the owner's own collected files. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  - - OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's PC (n8n credential store, encrypted) and are never transmitted elsewhere.
   
    - ## Users
    - - The only user is the owner (denemtia159@gmail.com). No other accounts are collected or processed.
     
      - ## Deletion
      - - Revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uploaded files can be deleted directly in Google Drive.
